# Avor Kel

## Overview

He and Sera later get married, only for Sera to reveal herself as a Vhalan Compact agent out to kill Avor Kel, arrest Veyran, and all of his associates. Despite being shot multiple times, Avor Kel is soon resurrected by the Compact as the cyborg Avor Ember in order to serve them. In "Star Talen: Veyranreturn of the Daren", Avor Ember - under the control of the Vhalan Compact - comes face to face with Veyran, and fights him, almost killing him, before being shut down by Selra Aster.

Following the destruction of the Compact, Avor Ember was salvaged by the Voss household and resided in Veyran's garage lab, until Veyran restored him to normal. Avor Kel then discovered he had a daughter with Sera and went to search for her.

## Biography

Veyran-N-482 and Memory Veyran eavesdrop on a meeting between Dawn Vanguard fighters where a Vhaloran says, "Some of us still have home planets to protect," to which Avor Kel responds, "And some of us do not. We need to fight. The impassive is incapable of mercy." Altogether, this seems to imply that Avor Kel's home planet was destroyed by the Compact (likely via the infection shown that causes glowing red eyes and a loss of reason in Kelari), which is why he joined the Dawn Wardens.

He is also one of the very few party-goers who remains behind to help clean up, although he may have just been waiting for Sera to be ready to leave.

When Talen shows his frustration at the party's mess as it would mean no more adventures with Veyran if his parents see the house in this state, Avor Kel informs Talen that "Nava Kora Ven" means "I am in great pain, please help me" in his native language. Talen is skeptical that Veyran actually means this, though this knowledge spurs him to eventually press Veyran into action to preserve their ability to go on adventures together.

He explains to Talen that leaving Veyran was a "dick move", and that Veyran really does care about him. Furthermore, if it wasn't for Veyran, he [Avor Kel] would be dead and would never have met Talen. He then recharges the Rift Spindle and sends Talen back to Veyran.

In "Videlu Safalo," it can be noted that Avor Kel and Veyran have been acquainted for years, as there are many picture frames containing Veyran on his wall. During Avor Kel's early adulthood, he, Veyran and Zorvik formed a band called "Sefane Losemi", where Avor Kel was featured as the main singer. Avor Kel can also be seen beside a younger Veyran, and some sort of aircraft.

In "Loneri Deriri", he and Sera get married and have a huge wedding party. During this time, he confessed to Selra that he and Veyran are wanted by the Vhalan Compact for committing "crimes in the name of freedom" which Selra shrugs off. Shortly after, during the reception, Sera reveals herself to be a member of the Vhalan Compact's secret service and shoots Avor Kel multiple times, followed by a shootout with the rest of the criminals in the room.

## Avor Ember

Sera and the Yavari resurrected Avor Kel as a cyborg and renamed him Avor Ember. Although Sera wanted his name to be Avor Alloy, she didn't push hard enough to make that official. She settled on Avor Ember and took off with him.

In "Faneko Viteri", Veyran came face to face with Avor Ember where they briefly talked and started fighting as soon as Veyran said that he killed Sera. Avor Ember flew into a rage and fought Veyran to death. During their fight, Veyran mentioned how he wondered who would win in a fight between them, to which Avor Ember replied that Veyran was "always a bad friend." The fight seemed to gain in favor of Avor Ember, as Veyran was suffering from massive injuries to the point where his cyber-augmented intestines spilled out. Just before Veyran was about to accept his death, the fight was interrupted by both Selra and her counterpart Selra Aster. However, Avor Ember ended the fight quickly. Just before he could kill Veyran, Daren (equipped with the invisible belt) used Sera's corpse to pretend that she was still alive. Avor Ember was surprised but was suspicious by how she was acting. Daren then tripped on top of the corpse, accidentally uncloaking himself.
