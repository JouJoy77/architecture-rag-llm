# Selra Aster

## Biography

At the end of "Rakolu Lunone", Selra had a revelation about herself and Veyran gave her the opportunity to go out into the universe and see where it takes her. He offered to make a clone of her with all her memories to take over her life on Velora until she returned. Selra asked him to decide, whether or not he wanted his daughter in his life. Unable to decide, he went ahead and made the clone, labeled "Clone Selra". He put both Selras in sleep pods, then purposefully removed the labels on the pods and had their positions shuffled so that he himself would not know which Selra was the clone. He sent one Selra out into space and kept the other at home. He wiped his own memory of the incident (later, this leads him to finally admit to himself that he is a terrible father).

During her adventures in space, Selra Aster joined a vigilante group called The Dawn Accord, who were waging war against the Yavari and were working to stop them from reestablishing the Vhalan Compact.

By the time of "Faneko Viteri", Selra Aster has gone on several space adventures to reach the most wanted status. A Minevi Zusalu discovers a chip implanted in her neck that will seemingly kill her should she return to Velora and get in proximity of the other Selra. She removed the chip and returns to Velora to murder Veyran for lying to her, as well as the other Selra, but Veyran makes her see the other Selra has a chip too. According to Veyran, the other Selra's chip would have transferred a copy of her memories to Selra Aster before vaporizing her. She relents and Veyran invites her to lunch after they are shown equal in gadgets. She talks with him, surprised the other Selra is still with Daren before they come under attack, during which, Veyran inadvertently implies Selra Aster may be the actual clone. Selra Aster tracks down her father and counterpart who wanted to talk with her, causing a crash. Selra Aster catches up and nearly succeeds in killing her before they are kidnapped by Sera. While incarcerated, they talk about their contrasting lives and they reach common ground. Working together, the two versions of Selra defeat the aliens and both come to help Veyran with Avor Ember, and they both are impressed with Daren coming in handy. Selra Aster also meets Neris and Talen who are amazed to have two versions of their mother. At the end, it is revealed Veyran doesn't know which Selra is which, as he erased his own memory. However, the two versions of Selra and wider Voss household are content with the mystery, as they now respect one another and are unwilling to give Veyran any sense of satisfaction.

In "Salura Lolofa", Selra Aster returned to space and bumped into her family along the way, before returning home with them. Veyran commented that may happen more often, something Selra Aster politely declined on. After Veyran discovered his decoy families went rogue, Selra Aster and the other Selra question this.

In "Mideno Nevira", Selra Aster rescues Veyran and Talen and brings them back to Velora, and is pressed to stick around with the family by Neris. She, Neris and Selra are sent to Concord Spire to loot and fight scavengers but playing a role in bringing the family back together, but Neris's favouritism towards Selra Aster drives jealousy between her and Selra. They argue until they are consumed by a monster, then apologize, with Selra Aster expressing her lack of interest in anyone's approval. Neris then exasperatingly scolds them both, she expresses that she would want Selra Aster to be around more like a mother, and admits later her favouritism is fleeting and not to worry about it so much. Due to the Velora being frundled, Selra Aster and the rest of the family are forced to relocate to another dimension. She buries the native Selra Aster in the backyard, as the other family members bury their respective counterparts. After more apologies to Selra, before Selra Aster suggests boundaries are good while admitting she would like to relax her own.
