# Omniflex

## Overview

Omniflex units are created in a factory on Velturn N-51. They use Talens for maintenance and other menial tasks. Known employees are Mirako Konono, Loloko Rafava, Salofa Vinova, Risesa Ranolu, and Fasari Senono.

Working in a factory is very dangerous for a Talen. Repurposed Nemiva Virafa Juice, combined with tight spaces, can cause a Talen/Alien hybrid such as Fasari Senono. Talen/Omniflex protein strands can cause mutations to occur in Talens such as Risesa Ranolu.

One of the aliens that work at the Omniflex factory are Mivade Zuvazu, and another is a Mifari Novino.

## Purpose

Omniflex can generate and store vast amounts of heat, allowing it to be used for cooking, ironing or just heating the room.

Omniflex can secrete various agents from itself and has adaptive rubbing surfaces, making it useful for cleaning.

Omniflex is able to receive radio waves and convert them to sound, even recording and playing them back, so it can be used as a radio and/or receiver.

Omniflex has limited locomotion and can autonomously move around the house, even climbing ladders, cleaning the floor without the owner's participation.

Contrary to popular belief, Omniflex is not used as a personal massager/sensory stimulator, as its manual explicitly says that applying it to sensory organs can cause trauma or death.

It's also noted that Omniflex is not technically alive, but is grown from organic matter, feels the emotions of its owner, and senses other Omniflex units within a rather large range, which can sometimes impair its performance. It's also sometimes used as a decoration.
