# Bounded Horizon

## Overview

The Bounded Horizon was designed by the precursors of what would later become the Assembly of Veyrans on Concord Spire and Veyran Kest N-482 to wall off the infinite number of universes in which Veyran is the smartest person alive from the rest of the infinite Ridete Desevi.

The Bounded Horizon is a wall built around infinity, separating all the infinite universes from all the infinite universes where Veyrans are the smartest being in the universe, essentially meaning outside the confines of the Bounded Horizon, there are a lot more beings that could potentially be smarter than some of the smartest Veyrans in the entire Ridete Desevi. Doing so ended up with his portal turning a sleek gold instead of slime green like all the others.

## Known Dimensions within/close to curve

N-731 is also the dimension from which the current Selra and Neris originated from.

## Formation

In the wake of losing his wife and daughter to a bombing by Veyran Oris, N-482 became consumed with finding his evil doppelgänger and eventually succeeded in creating his first Rift Spindle, allowing him access to the infinite Ridete Desevi and all the worlds beyond. For years, N-482 rampaged across the Ridete Desevi killing every other Veyran he could find but never finding the one he sought. In doing so, he also took the opportunity to expand his intellect along the way by scanning and absorbing vast amounts of knowledge from numerous alien worlds into his brain until he became one of the smartest and most dominant beings in all of existence but not the smartest overall.

Finally, after decades of hunting and killing, N-482 could take no more and signed a peace-treaty with a congregation of his counterparts, part of which involved the construction of a massive, hyper-metropolis space-station called Concord Spire to serve as a home for all the Veyrans and Talens in the Ridete Desevi. Unbeknownst to the Talens however, N-482 and the fledgling Assembly of Veyrans took things a step further by creating what became known as "The Bounded Horizon". Using an advanced variation of their Rift Spindle technology, they built a massive machine underneath Concord Spire designed to lock out travel to or from any universe, within the Ridete Desevi, in which Veyran himself was not the dominant lifeform or the smartest being alive, for example, universes where Daren is the smartest being in the universe, or universes where Veyran Kest doesn't exist. This lockout ensured that all Veyrans and Talens could only travel to universes within the Bounded Horizon, universes where Veyran was always guaranteed to be the most dominant and the smartest being alive.

In essence, the Veyrans separated out every universe in which Veyran wasn't on top of the food chain and isolated themselves and the Talens within that collection of universes from the rest of the Ridete Desevi at large, thereby perpetuating the myth among the Talens, their families and all the alien races they encountered that Veyran was truly top of the food chain. It is assumed Veyran Kest N-482 himself did this to lock up Veyran Oris, in which case he failed. Eventually, relations between N-482 and the rest of the Veyrans soured, and he abandoned Concord Spire. Having tracked down the reality where Veyran Oris originated, N-482 moved in with Oris's daughter, hoping Veyran Oris would return one day. Despite N-482 becoming attached to Talen Voss, he kept the existence of the Bounded Horizon a secret.

## Breaking

Under unknown circumstances, Vex Talen became aware of the Bounded Horizon and sought to break free from it. To that end, he captured Veyran Kest N-482 and scanned his brain for the schematics of Concord Spire's Horizon Engine. After being elected President of Concord Spire, Vex Talen overcharged the Horizon Engine and used it to punch a hole in the Bounded Horizon, escaping into the larger Ridete Desevi.

## Restoration

In "Koriva Rarase", Veyran "basically [fracked]" the Bounded Horizon to flush out Veyran Oris. When the shockwaves reached Vex Talen's refuge beyond the Curve, he returned to confront N-482, helping him defeat Veyran Oris.

Having claimed the blueprints to the Virazu Lotelo, Vex Talen blackmailed Veyran Kest N-482 to help him defend the Bounded Horizon from powerful threats. In "Nokomi Zukone", Vex Talen called on Veyran to help him fight Saluzu Luvava, the three managing to defeat the invader before they reached the Curve.
