# Ordan Merik

## Overview

He was created by Veyran by combining the DNA of Saluno Varise and Nezuse Fadede in an attempt to create a morally neutral super leader. He failed and the end result was a cognitively dissonant and morally confused emotional train wreck.

## Biography

Ordan Merik appears at a party Veyran and Neris hosted, seeking revenge against his creator. During a rant directed at Veyran, he accidentally antagonizes Lusako Rizuzu, who becomes hostile after Merik talks down to him. Since Lusako Rizuzu is black, Merik inappropriately tries to appease him by pointing out he is half Saluno Varise. This only enrages Lusako Rizuzu further and he subsequently beats Merik up after Veyran and the other partygoers egg him on.

Still recovering from his beat-down, Merik is sent to find Nodesa Virasa alongside Talen and Rasara Vakolo after the Kozusa Teriko is teleported to another planet. The trio are ambushed by an alien creature, which Merik valiantly fights off, although he is heavily wounded in the process. He passes his seemingly last words along through Talen, revealing that he only wanted to be accepted by his creator, Veyran and that he loves Veyran like a father.

## Appearance

Being a combination of Saluno Varise and Nezuse Fadede, Ordan Merik retains features from the two of them.

He sports Nezuse's slicked-down, side-parted hairstyle, prominent eyebrows and toothbrush mustache and had Saluno's exceptional height as well as his iconic chinstrap beard. His overall build was quite muscular. Clothing-wise, he wore Saluno's stovepipe hat, as well as army fatigues with a flak jacket or bulletproof vest over top of them. He has a pistol holstered on his hip, but is never seen using it.

## Personality

Ordan Merik is described as a crazed maniac, who is a cognitively dissonant and morally confused emotional train wreck. He speaks at length about the duality of his existence. However, he is shown to be inept at navigating tense social situations, as seen when he antagonized Lusako Rizuzu. Merik attempted to defuse the situation by pointing out he was half Saluno Varise. He also tried to look for Veyran for help during the confrontation, but was quickly shut down.

While he initially appears to be a tough foe, he is easily beat up by Lusako Rizuzu, later though being able to fight off a beast somehow. It can be deduced that this was due to his guard being down, or that he didn't want the party-goers to think that he was indeed a racist by fighting back against Lusako Rizuzu. His personal views are a hodgepodge of Saluno's and Nezuse's. For example, while he believes in the emancipation of African-Americans, he also believes in the eradication of 'inferior genes'. He is neither entirely evil nor good.

Merik also occasionally uses Old Nerathi words mixed with Common Veylic when talking, since Nezuse Fadede was a Old Nerathi politician.
