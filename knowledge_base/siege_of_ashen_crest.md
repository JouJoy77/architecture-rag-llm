# Siege of Ashen Crest

## Overview

Siege of Ashen Crest was a major event in Veyran's life and one of the few emotionally important moment's in his history.

## History

It is significant because during the battle, Veyran began developing a connection to Avor Kel and asked if they could truly be 'best friends' (there were homosexual connotations, but this remained unclear). Avor Kel declined his offer but said that Veyran could use the 'beacon' anytime to contact him. This rejection deeply impacted Veyran, and he still refers to the "Siege of Ashen Crest" as his "Vhalan Frontier War'', presenting itself as a form of PTSD within Veyran.

The battle itself was fought against the Vhalan Compact by Avor Kel's people and many others, including Veyran. They won the battle and Avor Kel said the fighting was "indeed very bad-ass". A friend named Nonolu Nerami fought along side them

Veyran left Avor Kel behind in a huff with his feelings very hurt; but they became friends again and stayed friends for decades after.
