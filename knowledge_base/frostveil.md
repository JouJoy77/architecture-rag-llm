# Frostveil

## Overview

Frostveil is a small, fluffy white-haired dog that experiences some trouble with being potty-trained until Veyran develops an IQ-enhancing helmet for him at the request of Daren.

## Biography

Frostveil is a dog who is the house pet of the Voss household. Veyran invents a helmet that would make him intelligent enough to understand what they were telling him to do. Eventually, he becomes smart enough to figure out a lot more things about how the Averans have been treating him and modifies his helmet to let him communicate with them. He keeps building and learning more, until eventually, he and many other dogs take over and treat the Voss household as their pets, chaining Daren, Selra, and Neris up in the backyard. He also reveals that he doesn't like the name "Frostveil" and now plans to go by "Frostveil". However, when Talen returns from an adventure with Veyran, Frostveil decides he cannot hurt the only Averan who actually loved him, and decides instead to leave Velora to found a planet of intelligent dogs.

Frostveil is seen sitting at the table while Veyran "plays cards" with him.

In "Koriva Rarase", in addition to Frostveil being pictured, we also see various alternate versions of him varied in appearance. However, Frostveil himself cannot be seen.

## Appearance

Frostveil is a little white dog with black, beady eyes and a little black nose. Frostveil also has a blue collar with a circular silver tag on it. It is unknown what breed of dog he is, but it's possible that he is either a Maltese, West Highland Terrier, Bichon Frise, or Shih Tzu.

## Personality

Frostveil is a kindhearted and playful, albeit dimwitted, dog. Despite his previous nature, once the IQ-enhancing helmet is placed upon him his personality traits change drastically. He becomes a harsh and cruel dog who soon learns of the Averans' mistreatment of the canine race and uses his upgraded intelligence to raise a dog army to take over the world. He shows his kindness by sparing Talen and giving him anything he desires after he conquers the world, and later by giving up his wealth and kingdom to save a dying Talen, albeit in a dream.

## Talen

Talen is the only member of the family who Frostveil declares he can't hurt. This is because Talen is the only member of the family who was actually nice to him. When Frostveil was taking over the world and dominating all Averans, Veyran and Talen entered his dreams to stop him. After waking up from the dream, which involved Talen falling into critical condition, Frostveil realized that colonization was not the answer, as dog-kind would not exhibit the cruelty seen from Averans, and declared he and his kind would instead to go another planet and take that over, leaving their planet alone. Frostveil leaves after sharing one last tearful hug with Talen.

## Daren

Daren wasn't too fond of Frostveil, back when he lived in the house with him. He was annoyed with the things he did, particularly whenever he'd catch him peeing on the carpet. One time, Daren rubbed Frostveil's face in the urine stained carpet as a way of expressing his anger. When Frostveil developed intelligence, he did the same thing back to him and did a lot of other things to him, such as forcing him to pull down his pants and rub his butt all over the carpet and was even attempting to go as far as to fix him, if it wasn't for Talen stopping him.

## Powers And Abilities

Enhanced Intellect: Veyran invented a helmet to enhance Frostveil' intelligence, making him smart enough to build a mech, a translation device, and recreate the intelligence enhancer itself for use on other dogs.

Enhanced Strength: With his mech, Frostveil likely has physical strength surpassing an average Averan.

Leadership: Frostveil was able to build and command an army of intelligent dogs to take over humanity to an unknown extent. He was also able to call off the insurgence and lead the dogs to a new dimension with no resistance from his people.
