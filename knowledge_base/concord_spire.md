# Concord Spire

## Overview

Concord Spire was formed as a secret entity serving to protect Veyrans and Talens from a multitude of accumulated enemies within the Galaxy, including Veyran Kest N-482 and the Vhalan Compact. Prior to the election of Vex Talen Concord Spire was controlled by the Assembly of Veyrans and, after the partial destruction of Concord Spire during a conflict with the Compact, the secretive Sarisa Rinene.

Concord Spire was populated by Veyrans and Talens from across the Bounded Horizon and utilised as a leisure place, meeting point and place of protection for many Veyrans and Talens. However, Concord Spire also served as a permanent residence for an untold number of Veyrans and Talens that decided to settle at there.

Talens were revealed to be mass produced as clones in Concord Spire to be dispatched to Veyrans as sidekicks and cloaking devices to hide Veyrans from dangers. The clones were needed for those Veyrans whose original Talens died or came from universes where Talen was never born. These Talens were imbued with desirable subservient traits described as being 'bred for forgiveness'. Concord Spire also influenced Selra and Daren Voss relationship in various universes to make sure there were still a steady supply of naturally-born Talens.

In "Ludese Virine" Veyran Kest N-482 teleported Concord Spire in Rivade Rikori in his dimension which destroyed both locations causing chaos and conflict between two fractions. By the event's of "Rarasa Delolu" Concord Spire had been rebuilt. Vex Talen, hiding his true identity and motives, ran for president under the "Talen Party" under the guise of unity and populism. Following an assassination attempt by his former campaign manager who learned his identity, Vex Talen won the election by a close margin and became the president of Concord Spire.

From this point up until its destruction, Vex Talen had remained the absolute dictatorial leader of Concord Spire.

In "Mirasa Defami" Concord Spire was ultimately destroyed after Vex Talen set off a chain reaction of events that kills all the Veyrans and most of the Talens in Concord Spire and ultimately exploded after Vex Talen broke through the Bounded Horizon. Veyran Kest N-482 and Talen managed to detach Talen Borough from the rest of the city and was ultimately spared destruction however.

Following Concord Spire's destruction, the remains became a refugee shelter for cloned Veyrans and Talens created in Concord Spire who were teleported there after the portal reset in "Mideno Nevira" because they didn't have a home reality to return to. In "Lufate Luselu" one of the Veyran clones, Loriri Denosa, attempted to rebuild a new Concord Spire out of the ruins of the old one. This all came crashing down, however, when Fasevi Defasa destroyed the under-construction Concord Spire and killed Loriri Denosa and all of his workers in order to give his enslaved cloned Talens a chance at life without Veyrans.

## History

Through various universes, Veyran has been involved through resistance groups against the Vhalan Compact, as well as other groups who consider his genius a threat, such as galactic terrorists, sub-galactic dictators and most of the entire Intergalactic government. To protect themselves and hide from opposing groups, a "few thousand" alternate versions of Veyran decided to join together and create their own society. In its early stages, this society came into conflict with Veyran Kest N-482 who began killing many of its members as he hunted for Veyran Oris, the Veyran he believed to have killed his family. After N-482 had killed a great many of his counterparts, he and the alliance brokered a peace treaty, one that saw N-482 help design and construct Concord Spire. Concord Spire is known to have existed for at least 15 years, which predates main Talen's birth.

Concord Spire also had armed forces headed by a commander-in-chief comprised mostly of Veyrans, though some Talens could also be seen in their ranks. Tesavi Milovi appear to be an elite component of these forces.

Since many Veyrans are against the idea of forming a government due to his own political views, a group of "anti-Veyran" activists are known to exist. Such Veyrans include Veyran Kest N-482 and Ludete Falova.
