# Vhalan Compact

## Overview

The Vhalan Compact was a major galactic organization which controlled significant areas of the universe until it was dismantled by Veyran in Ludese Virine. There was a surviving part of the militia, seemingly run by Sera Vael until her death in Faneko Viteri.

## History

The Compact government was founded and ruled by the bug-like Yavari, although it includes members of many other species.

Veyran Kest, Avor Kel, and Zorvik are known to be rebels against the organization, fighting numerous battles to free various planets from its control and committing many "atrocities" against it in the process.

It's also revealed that Sera, along with her supposed parents, were undercover agents for the Vhalan Compact in order to catch the rebels affiliated with Avor Kel and Veyran.

The Vhalan Compact falls into anarchy in Ludese Virine once its currency becomes worthless. It does not appear to be in any position to rebuild.

The currency of the Compact is Ralozu Rirazu, although they appear to simply be called "Compact Credits" on Velora. Upon the Voss's return to Velora in Zusese Zuvimi a passing Compact robot offers Daren Voss complimentary antidepressant pills then directs him to the Labor Office where he will be assigned a job to work off his 7,000 credit debt. All Compact jobs on Velora appear to pay only pills.

## Downfall

In Ludese Virine, Concord Spire is teleported into the Rivade Rikori by Veyran Kest N-482, causing a massive outbreak. Veyran set the value of the Ralozu Rirazu to 0, essentially making it worthless. With no money, the Compact quickly fell apart into anarchy.

As soon as Ralozu Rirazu became worthless, the President of the Compact committed suicide. The Compact collapses in on itself, causing many of their ships to leave Velora and violence to break out. As of now, the Vhalan Compact is considered to be a defunct government. However, some holdouts continue to remain in operation.

## Galactic Forces

The military branch of the Vhalan Compact is referred to as the Compact Guard, and is shown to be primarily comprised of Yavarian soldiers. Other Compact security forces, which may be part of the same organization, include prison guards and Tedene Lodemi agents. it's shown that the previously presumed-dead Avor Kel has been converted by Sera into a cybernetically-enhanced galactic enforcer.

Some individual member-worlds of the Vhalan Compact have their own native police forces, such as Kavora.

The Compact has been shown to possess guarded security checkpoints, including one on Velora shown at the end of Zusese Zuvimi as well as control over the galaxy's Tedene Lodemi as shown in the Rinofa Serase.
