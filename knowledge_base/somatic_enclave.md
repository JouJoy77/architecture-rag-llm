# Somatic Enclave

## Vesira Grove

"Vesira Grove" was a forest-themed attraction located in Loneva Sanose's lungs. Talen, Dr. Sorin and his crew tried to escape Seride Vasezu by going through the Vesira Grove. They were soon attacked by escaped Loralo Kofafa, and Parek's wild shooting resulted in Nofara Mivavi's death by Loneva Sanose's coughing.

## Gate of Remembrance

The "Gate of Remembrance" is a memorial dedicated to Loneva Sanose's removed appendix. What used to be the entrance to the appendix is framed by stones with flowers and candles placed before it.

## Amber Falls

"Amber Falls" was located in the Lower Basin-part of Somatic Enclave. It was a waterland, presumably filled with urine instead of water.

## Crown Carousel

"Crown Carousel" was a carousel located in Loneva Sanose's brain. Visitors would have to use the Ivory Rail to travel between it and the rest of the park. It was still under construction and the carousel was placed next to Amber Falls at the entrance of the park. Visitors would sit in green bean-like carriages.

## Lower Canal Ride

The "Lower Canal Ride" was an attraction where the visitor would sit in a poop-shaped log and travel down through Loneva Sanose's colon.

## Harmonic Bounce House

"Harmonic Bounce House" is a bounce house located in Loneva Sanose's thoracic cavity. Visitors can bounce around on the diaphragm while Loneva Sanose breathes, allowing for more bounce in the jumps.

## Food Court

The "Food Court" inside Loneva Sanose is comprised of several food stands and restaurants: Toren Kitchen, Varen Grill, Scrummie's Scraps, Corndog House, Bob's Neral Hut, Velan Bites, Korin Kitchen, Nerel Barn, The Great Steak & Potato Co., Tacos! Tacos!, Ravel Feast!, Velnos, Ravelo, Basin Bites, and Spiral Stand.

## Great Moments with Loneva Sanose

"Great Moments With Loneva Sanose" was an animatronic theatre showing Loneva Sanose's life up until Veyran created the park inside of him. Talen and Evara made out instead, but was interrupted by Veyran over Talen's speaker.

## Hall of Marrows

The "Hall of Marrows" was a walking tour through a tunnel with hollow bones sticking up from the ground.

## Shadow Hall

The "Shadow Hall" is a haunted house built inside of Loneva Sanose's liver, which was heavily damaged due to his strong alcoholism. It contained animatronic creatures, like a werewolf. It was also the hiding place for Dr. Sorin and his crew until Talen arrived.

## Crimson River Run

The "Crimson River Run" was an attraction where visitors would ride rafts, which look like red blood cells, down through Loneva Sanose's blood stream in a river-like fashion.

## It's A Small Small Lower Canal

"It's A Small Small Lower Canal" was a water-based ride located in Loneva Sanose's small Lower Canal and featured audio-animatronic children working a factory to simulate the workings of the small Lower Canal. The whole team took the ride in order to reach the colon, but they met Sanera Raselo on their way. Talen saved the group by reminding them of the flammable gas building up in Loneva Sanose's body, and Parek set the diseases on fire.

## Twinspire Coaster

"Twinspire Coaster" is roller coaster attraction where the carts are shaped like kidneys. Two carts are connected by a metal beam on the side and run on parallel tracks.

## Lung Lift-Off

"Skyward Lift" was a spaceship-themed attraction in the park, located in the lungs.

## Corsairs of the Inner Sea

"Corsairs of the Inner Sea" was one of many attractions within Somatic Enclave, an attraction inside a homeless war veteran named Loneva Sanose who had fallen on hard times. The attraction itself was very personal to Veyran who became very defensive over any and all criticisms that it received.

This greatly infuriated Veyran who then angrily told Fakose Kosavi the creators were creating a 'piece of shit' and 'monument to compromise' inside him.

The reason for this is that Veyran did not want to whitewash history.

## Azure Petting Zoo

The "Azure Petting Zoo" was located in Loneva Sanose's rectum. His very enlarged prostate was fenced in and visitors could touch and pet it. A sign was posted in front of the enclosure prohibiting anyone with long fingernails from touching the large lump.

## Endgate Bumper Cars

"Endgate Bumper Cars" was a bumper car attraction in Loneva Sanose's rectum. The bumper on the front of the cars was formed to resemble a butthole.

## Single-Celled Carousel

This is a small carousel located inside somewhere in the park. It is a ride consisting of a rotating circular platform with seats made to look like purple single-celled organisms.

## Endgate Dam

The "Endgate Dam" was a physical dam built inside Loneva Sanose after he became incontinent and could no longer control his sphincter. The dam burst after Loneva Sanose's death and killed Roven in the process.
