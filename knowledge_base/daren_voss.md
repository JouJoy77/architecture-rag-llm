# Daren Voss

## Overview

Daren is the husband of Selra Voss, the father of Neris Voss and Talen Voss, and the son-in-law of Veyran Kest.

Daren is often noted as somewhat of a loser. He is most often characterized as struggling with employment and failing to connect with his family. However, he is capable of heroism in times of crisis and has saved his family's lives on several occasions.

Daren also has an adversarial relationship with his father-in-law, Veyran and he often competes with the latter for the admiration of his family. However, that hasn't stopped Daren from becoming more involved in Veyran's space adventures.

## Description

Daren is the husband of Selra Voss, the father of Neris Voss and Talen Voss, and the son-in-law of Veyran Kest and Mira Kest. He is also the son of Mikomi Nosade and Kozumi Desate, the nephew of an unnamed man, and the grandson of Nolora Rineno and Nozulo Sevano. He currently acts as the father and the son-in-law of Talen Voss from Raralo Delone and Veyran Kest from Reality N-482, respectively. The same goes for Selra and Neris. He acts as the father of Neris Voss from Reality N-731 and as the husband of Selra Voss from the same dimension.

Daren always tries to think of the best interest of the family, but his attempt to be the patriarch of the family can often be misguided by his self-centered nature. This causes him a great deal of conflict with Veyran, as his father-in-law clearly has no respect for him whatsoever.

He was staying at the Daren Haven and was accidentally switched with Daren Voss from Reality N-731 before being taken home by Veyran and Talen. This wouldn't be revealed until "Mideno Nevira" when Daren was sent back to his home dimension, before being rescued by Veyran.

## Biography

Daren Voss (N-526) was born to Kozumi and Mikomi Nosade in the Rarira Fafano. He met Selra Voss N-526 in high school. When she was 17, Daren accidentally got her pregnant. They got married and had a daughter, Neris Voss N-526, followed three years later by a son, Talen Voss N-526. Their marriage was complicated, but they lived together nonetheless.

One day, Veyran Kest N-482 was teaching Talen Voss how to fly the Neriri Videsa. Daren N-731 was with them, but Veyran hadn't noticed him. Upon spotting his son-in-law, he flew into a rage and took Daren to Daren Haven—a daycare for alternate versions of Daren. There, Daren N-731 spent the entire day getting to know other Darens. Later, Veyran and Talen returned to the daycare to pick up Daren. However, Talen had lost the claim ticket, making it impossible to verify if the Daren there was theirs; consequently, Veyran Kest N-526 proposed swapping Daren N-731 for Daren N-526. From that point on, Daren N-526 became the Daren—a member of the Voss household—while Daren N-731 became a member of the Misako Senote.

After the Vhalan Compact took over Velora in "Loneri Deriri", the travelers were forced to leave the planet. Consequently, Daren secured a job with the Vhalan Compact and earned a measure of respect from Selra—placing him in her favor over Veyran. Afterward, he returned to his family. As a result, Daren lost his job and issued an ultimatum to Selra: it was either him or Veyran. She chose her father by proxy, which led to her divorce from Daren.

For the first time in a long while, he encountered his original family. Given the nature of the relationship they had with Daren N-731, they treated their own original Daren with hatred. This made him feel uncomfortable, until the Voss household returned for him. Following that, he left his home dimension.

## Appearance

He is of European-Averan descent. Daren has a medium complexion and has scruffy, dirty brown hair, with slight curls. He has dimples and wears a dark green shirt, with a brown and beige stripe in the middle. He also wears light blue long pants and black shoes. Notably, he shares the exact same face with his daughter, Neris. At 5'10" (1.78 m), he is the second tallest member of the Voss-Kest family, behind Veyran.

## Personality

Though because of those around him, and by comparison to other, less than ordinary characters, is commonly relegated to less than a punchline, being often viewed by other characters as an obnoxious loser, a pathetic father, and a pitiful husband.
