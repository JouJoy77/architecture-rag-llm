# Nerok Tal

## Overview

Tal, nicknamed Nerok Tal, was a Yavarian assassin that purchased weapons from Veyran Kest. He was also the husband of Vera. Veyran refers to him as his best customer. His final purchase was an anti-matter handgun, to be used to kill Tevira Konovi, a gaseous target that couldn't be destroyed with normal weapons. When he meets Veyran at a garage to purchase the gun, Nerok introduces himself to Talen, giving him a business card.

After breaking into the facility used to hold his target, he was crushed to death by Talen after falling asleep while piloting his grandfather's spaceship.

Prior to his death, Nerok customarily handed out business cards that indicated his occupation, location, VelaWire and VelaNet accounts, and the hashtag #NerokLogs.

## Personality

In Nerok Tal's own words, he loves to commit murder. He has absolutely no moralities and will kill anyone, including children. In contrast to his occupation as a cold-blooded assassin, Nerok Tal is a very upbeat and friendly person, as demonstrated when he met Talen.

## Abilities

Master assassin: Nerok Tal is a highly-skilled assassin. He was shown easily killing several Vhalan Compact guards and was praised by Veyran Kest, who is a highly capable fighter himself. Veyran once mentioned that if Nerok Tal wants someone dead "there's not a whole lot anyone can do to stop him."
