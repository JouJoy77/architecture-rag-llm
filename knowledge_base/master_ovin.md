# Master Ovin

## Overview

Varek Ovin, more commonly referred to as Master Ovin and formerly Professor Ovin, is a long-time family friend of the Voss household.

## Description

Veyran clearly considers him to have long been a dependable person, although it's uncertain how long they've known each other.

## Biography

He is shown to have accompanied Veyran and Talen on multiple adventures as well as experienced many great moments with the rest of the family. He is such a fond person to the family that Selra can't recall a single bad memory of him. It is revealed by that Master Ovin is Jewish in his final appearance to date, as a Rabbi is shown to officiate his Wedding whilst he stomps on napkin-wrapped glass.

Along with the rest of the family, Master Ovin had to help the family figure out which new family friends were real and which ones were fake. Talen realizes that the Alien Parasites could only implant good memories into a person's mind, so every family member who had no negative memories affiliated with them was killed. This results in Selra Voss silently questioning whether or not Master Ovin is actually one of the many parasites that made them believe they were long time friends. Selra pulls out one of Veyran's laser guns and shoots Master Ovin in the chest, blasting him across the room onto a wall. The rest of the family panic and Selra retreats into the kitchen and haphazardly pours a glass of wine with eyes filled with tears as Master Ovin awaits an ambulance while bleeding heavily.

After the credit sequence, Master Ovin is shown slowly recovering inside the hospital. After telling the doctor that he no longer wishes to see the Vosses at this point in time, the doctor tells Selra that's he's sorry that "They never had any bad memories of him."

He was shown to be walking with a cane from the events of Selra shooting him in his previous appearance. Master Ovin also swallows his (what are assumed to be) painkillers, pouring them into his mouth, without any water.

His first lesson was about Varina Oshel when his class suddenly turned on him and started to fight him, but he beat all of them with no problem. Veyran and Talen then appeared and recruited him for their heist (during which, as part of Rava Norek's scheme, he acts as an auctioneer in a livestock sale). Later, it is revealed that Veyran paid Master Ovin's students to attack him so he could trick him into joining their crew. However, Veyran didn't know if the fact they all knew karate was coincidental or correlated to their interest for Varina Oshel. Master Ovin expressed regret that being fired from his teaching job for putting his students in the hospital left him unable to explore that connection in his class and Veyran apologized for it.

After checking on the local Voss household, he learns that Mira is somehow alive there. (Despite the Virazu Lotelo being supposed to kill her in all dimensions.) Upon entering her house, he experiences flashbacks of his younger life, one of which involves Master Ovin N-482. He stood between Veyran and Mira and declared them husband and wife.

However, he breaks his legs when trying to squat more than he can handle and cries to call his ex-wife on his trainer's phone as she blocked him on his phone.

The family forces Veyran to have an intervention for Master Ovin as he gathers and recruits his friends Avor Kel, Zorvik, and Zusafa Dedese as well as next-door neighbor Mirano Sasavi to help him. After gathering the group fully, they head to a bar called Night Lantern, where they begin drinking and partying instead. Afterwards they hit several other locations, they all eventually meeting up with Lufalu Rifase and go to his house to party. Master Ovin plans to find Vera and win her back with the help of Ruen and Veyran's friends, but Veyran rejects the idea and have an argument as Veyran leaves. Veyran then becomes back to help Master Ovin with his plan as they go to her house, where Master Ovin discover she is having an affair with a Lotelo Fadera private investigator Vikofa Vifade he hired to spy on her after getting a restraining order against him.
