# The Confluence

## Overview

The Confluence is a hivemind and Veyran Kest's former lover. They take the forms of multiple races, sexes, and species, and use both male and female avatars around Veyran.

## Biography

After Veyran, Talen and Neris follow a distress signal coming from a seemingly 'abandoned' ship, a group of Lusazu Rilolo ask for Veyran's help to defeat the evil entity that's taking over their minds. Using the assimilated members of the crew, The Confluence takes control of the group's minds, and they recognize Veyran. Veyran introduces them to his grandchildren, and explains that he and The Confluence used to date.

The four of them return to Nonete Raride. The Confluence speaks with Veyran through its alien citizens, occasionally switching between avatars mid-conversation. They tell Veyran their plans to manage an organization efficient enough to merge with the Vhalan Compact, where they will have access to countless other worlds for them to assimilate until they become "what the single-minded once called a god". Veyran is impressed by this, and the two quickly resume their relationship as lovers.

While Veyran and The Confluence rekindle their relationship, The Confluence also takes care of Neris and Talen. The Confluence has so much fun reconnecting with Veyran and performing crazy stunts to impress him (especially making and ingesting Spiral Ash, a powerful drug) that they gradually lose control of their citizens.

After regaining their free will, the citizens of the planet start a race war, chasing after Talen and Neris until a The Confluence-led SWAT team rescues them. Neris and Talen, traumatized, insist that Veyran takes them all home. When Veyran opens a portal for them and insists on staying behind, Neris explains that Veyran has an unhealthy impact on The Confluence, and expresses her disapproval toward their relationship.

Veyran stays with The Confluence for a while longer, but after leaving to use the bathroom, emerges to find that The Confluence has deserted the city (and possibly the planet) and written breakup letters across every surface. They confess their love for Veyran, but ultimately concedes that they cannot be together. Veyran returns home and attempts suicide, but narrowly fails.

As shown from "Midevi Mivide", The Confluence still cares a lot for Veyran despite how they ended their relationship and tried to check up on him multiple times via call and later went to Velora when they heard that he had begun to hunt for "him" once again. When Veyran continually ignores them, they resort to the extreme by taking over the entire state of Zerath.

After Loloko Sazusa and Dr. Ivara investigate Zerath after hearing reports of a cult there, Veyran claims that he and The Confluence agreed that the latter would not take over Velora. Veyran and Loloko Sazusa secure the state using a dome and The Confluence approaches Velora in their space vessel to speak to them via video chat. The Confluence and Veyran get into an argument as The Confluence leaves despite their hivemind not properly released. After Loloko Sazusa takes over the hivemind, Veyran and Dr. Ivara meet The Confluence in their space vessel in order to get their help as Veyran and The Confluence get into another argument. Dr. Ivara sides with The Confluence as Veyran ignored their voicemails and tells them that Veyran is changing slowly in which The Confluence agrees to help them by reconnecting them. At the end, The Confluence releases everyone from their hivemind on Velora but does not trust Veyran anymore as Veyran leaves their space vessel due to ruining his chance to reconcile with them.

## Abilities

The Confluence assimilates others into the hivemind by using one of its hosts to vomit into another individual's mouth. The Confluence's hosts have been seen acting animalistic while spreading (screeching and pointing at unassimilated individuals), although The Confluence can imitate natural 'humanoid' behavior as well while in a passive, controlled state. It has individual control of everybody it assimilates. While many of The Confluence's hosts are shown to possess yellow sclerae, it has shown to be able to hide it to pose as single-minded.

All of The Confluence's avatars seem to possess the same brain while controlled by it, and The Confluence is able to move and speak transitionally through multiple planet-scale civilizations of people. However, Veyran's blue serum seems to remove its control, leaving The Confluence able to direct less and less bodies at once and making them dizzy.
