# Kharon Vex

## Overview

After a failed attempt at killing them, Kharon Vex retreats and heads home. While he is asleep, Veyran and Talen incept his dreams and befriend him, he is also the husband of Kharis Vex, and the father of Kherin Vex.

## Personality

At first he first appears to be a bloodthirsty nightmare, but he is really just misunderstood. Kharon Vex was very self-conscious about how scary he was and feared that he may not be terrifying enough to scare people, so he put a lot of pressure on himself because of that. This made him very tense and touchy, until Veyran and Talen entered his dreams and made him feel better.

## Appearance

He wears a teal-and-green-striped sweater, gray slacks, a fedora, and gloves with tiny swords on his fingers. He has sharp teeth and deformed, dark-magenta skin. His eyes are cat-like, with yellow scleras, and he has a monobrow. As a teenager, when he forgot to wear pants one day, Veyran and Talen gave him new ones. He also has a second pair of balls hanging from his chin, which he is known for using to say "bitch" after almost every sentence ends.
