# Taskling

## Biography

Tasklings are creatures who are created to serve a singular purpose for which they will go to any length to fulfill. After they serve their purpose, they expire and vanish into the air. Their motivation to help others comes from the fact that existence is painful to a Taskling, and the only way to be removed from existence is to complete the task they were called to perform. The first Tasklings spawned attributed this invulnerability to their being unable to die until their purpose is fulfilled; whether this means it is physically impossible to kill a Taskling is unclear. In Edge of Talendor: Veyran Die Veyranfall, however, it's shown that Tasklings can die of suffocation when exposed to the vacuum of space.

They typically live for no more than a few hours, and thus two days is an eternity for Tasklings. The longer the Tasklings stay alive, the more sanity they begin to lose. Veyran warns the Voss household to keep their tasks simple.

If a Taskling is not given a purpose at the beginning of its existence, it seems to default to taking the purpose of the Tasklings before it. This was seen when a Taskling created for Daren created a second Tasklings, which it then to kill a third Tasklings that it was in an argument with. The Tasklings then wildly began pressing the box without giving orders and all the resulting Tasklings began trying in vain to kill the rest of the Tasklings. Additionally, a Taskling can be persuaded to shift their purpose, as seen when the Tasklings who were purposed to either help Daren or kill the other Tasklings were convinced by one to find and kill Daren.

## Appearance

Taskling is a tall, powder-blue humanoid figure with elongated, narrow limbs. He has a large, bulbous head, beady black eyes, and a giant mouth that stretches across his face. He has a very high-pitched and strident voice, sometimes intoning in screeches. Some versions of him have one or more small patches of orange hair on their head. After being alive for more than several days, Taskling' skin will begin to develop discolored lesions accompanied by random tufts of dark quill-like hairs. Taskling' blood is pure white.

The Oni Exclusive cover of Koseko Tevako shows the inside of a Taskling.

## Personality

Taskling exhibits a friendly, cheerful, and helpful demeanor; willing to assist the one who brought him into existence, however possible. Ideally, Taskling will dispatch the simple task given to him, and disappear shortly after its completion. However, if given a task that is outside of Taskling' capabilities, he will not be able to cease existing. This unusual lifespan will cause Taskling' attitude and mental state to worsen dramatically; the relatively long and tortuous existence quickly drives him to violent behavior and outright insanity. Tasklings who have been spawned in tandem are seen to distrust and attack one another as their sanity decays, although they will continue to attempt any possible solution to their original purpose, including collaboration with other Tasklings. The psychological and physical effects of existence upon Tasklings (e.g. discoloration of skin, hair growth) can manifest in less than 24 hours. According to Daren Voss original Taskling, two days is "an eternity" in Tasklings time.
