# Prism Arcade

## History

Prism Arcade is an arcade themed building where Talen plays a virtual reality game called Risese Rirade, where he lives through the entire life of a man named Lutevi Zukoko up to the age of 55.

When Talen returned, he was furious at Veyran for selling a gun to the assassin Nerok Tal for a single afternoon at Prism Arcade. Veyran didn't care for what Talen had to say and proceeded to take his turn in Risese Rirade.

In the commercial an alien can be seen getting teleported into a game and getting shot and another alien can be seen getting decapitated.

In Vavimi Fasami, there are Minovi Luvalo machines located all over the various dimensions. Here you can buy Talens using Lomimi Serasa.

## Location

It is unknown where Prism Arcade is, but the ceiling window shows that it's in space rather than on a planet. The events of Lonemi Sefavi also suggest that it is somewhat near a Fazumi Videne and the garage in which Talen first meets Nerok Tal.
