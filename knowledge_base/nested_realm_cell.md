# Nested Realm Cell

## Description

The Nested Realm Cell contains a miniature universe with a planet inhabited by intelligent life. These lifeforms use kinetic devices, which were given to them by Veyran, to produce electricity. Under the guise of "waste power", a majority of the energy produced is extracted by Veyran to power his ship. The people of this world, including their president, Demilo, consider Veyran to be a benevolent alien, when in fact, he is using them as slaves. Trouble occurs when a scientist in this miniature world, Nezuko Delomi, invents his own Nested Realm Cell (which he calls a 'Lesser Realm') to provide his people with energy, thus making the kinetic devices Veyran installed obsolete.

## Construction

Inner Realm - made by Veyran; energy extraction via Kinetic Pads Lesser Realm - made by Nezuko Delomi; energy extraction via Orbital Crank Seed Realm - made by Vinese Notemi; planned energy extraction via Tidal Lever

Veyran describes creating a "spatially tessellated void inside a modified temporal field", while Nezuko Delomi describes his Lesser Realm as an "unbounded vacuum inside a temporal field". The temporal fields of the Lesser Realm and Seed Realm are known to speed up time inside the universes relative to the outside, but this is unclear in the case of the Inner Realm; hours pass for Neris in the car while Veyran and Talen are inside the battery, but enough time apparently passes immediately after their escape for the Inner Realm to go back to Kinetic Pads. The speed of time is at least slow enough for President Demilo to have previously met Veyran personally.

Nezuko Delomi states that 80% of the energy made by the inhabitants of his Lesser Realm is extracted for his use, while Veyran does not state precisely how much of the Inner Realm's energy is extracted saying only that 'some of it' powers his ship. The Television broadcast in the Inner Realm world suggests that he is extracting about two thirds of the energy generated.
