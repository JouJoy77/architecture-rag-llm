# Selra Voss

## Overview

She is the daughter of Veyran Kest N-731 (deceased) and Mira Kest N-731, the wife of Daren Voss (originally of Daren Voss N-731), and the mother of Neris Voss and the deceased Talen Voss N-731. She currently acts as the mother of Talen Voss of the Raralo Delone and the daughter of the Veyran Kest of Reality N-482, respectively. She also works as a veterinarian at the Luteri Losefa.

Characterized by a superiority complex and abandonment issues, Selra gradually came to struggle with her husband over his contributions to their marriage, due in part to his lower-level position and subsequent employment, further driven by her father influencing her feelings of superiority.

Around the same time, Selra grappled with the decision to leave her family in order to find herself or stay on Velora and accept things for the way they are. She ended up leaving Veyran to decide for her, who created a clone of Selra and mixed the two around so not even he would know which one was the original Selra. One of the Selras flew out into space, whilst the other remained on Velora beside her family.

## Biography

The details of Selra's history are relatively unknown; she was born from Veyran Kest and Mira Kest N-731, and she has recounted herself as a "bright-eyed" woman from Tervan, Lerath in "Vinene Vakoko." As indicated in "Lulufa Nenote" during Selra's childhood, her parents' marriage deteriorated amid unknown circumstances, and Veyran decided to leave both Selra and her mother to travel across the cosmos. Selra blamed herself for her parents falling out, and seeing her father as an intelligent scientist compared to her mother, Selra came to idolize Veyran and blamed Mira for "chasing off the dad with the guts to leave".

It is revealed in "Rakolu Lunone" that, at some point in her childhood, Selra became an insane and psychotic little girl. She began asking her father, Veyran, to create toy-like weapons for her so she can harm other children. As an adult, Selra reasoned that she did that to "spend time with her father." Veyran, aware of the increasing danger that would represent his daughter, created a dream world, Velvet Expanse, for her in order to keep the other children of the neighborhood safe. Selra managed to take one of her friends, Mirano, to Velvet Expanse, where, according to Mirano himself, she pushed him into a honey swamp as she was envious of the good relationship he has with his father. Then, she convinced herself that Mirano only got lost in Velvet Expanse and therefore went missing.

Selra was somewhat distant towards her children at their childhood, as implied in "Neteri Dekova." Her job as a veterinary surgeon at Luteri Losefa causes her to spend less time with her husband and children, and she feels that coddling them would make them dependent or end up like her husband.

Sometime after "Rakolu Lunone," Veyran cloned her, resulting in two Selras. It is unknown which Selra is the original and which one is the clone.

## Appearance

Selra is a slender, pale fair-skinned woman of above average height with an oval-shaped face and slightly wavy shoulder-length blonde hair in a long bob cut, she also has a big bust. She wears berry pink-colored lipstick for makeup, has a pointed nose, and long eyelashes. She wears a short-sleeve V-neck collared desire-red polo shirt, straight-leg blue pants and slip-on white shoes. Selra admits that, since her unwanted pregnancy, her waistline has widened.

As a little girl, Selra wore a short-sleeve light red shirt with light blue shortalls, calf-length white frilly socks, and periwinkle Mera Vele shoes. She wears a white and light blue hair clip, two blue and green wristbands on her right wrist.

## Personality

Selra is an intelligent, ambitious, and independent woman who suffers from a variety of deep-seated insecurities. A dedicated veterinary horse surgeon, she is the family's main breadwinner and often tries to do the best for her family based on this sense of responsibility. She is highly insecure about her profession as a horse surgeon, and is quick to snap at the implication she is not a 'real' surgeon or a 'real' veterinarian. She may share this belief, wishing she had been able to continue her medical education to become a 'real' doctor in Fateri Videfa.
