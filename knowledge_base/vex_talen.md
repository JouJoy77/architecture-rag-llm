# Vex Talen

## Overview

He is one of the many versions of Talen from across the Ridete Desevi, but unique in being notably amoral, ruthless and displaying intelligence rivalling that of Veyran. He initially appeared alongside Minovi Sadezu, who was hunting down and killing several Veyrans throughout the Ridete Desevi. It was later revealed Minovi Sadezu was merely being controlled by Vex Talen, although he remained undetected and travelled to Concord Spire by posing as one of the Talens liberated from Minovi Sadezu's lair.

He served as the first Talen to be democratically elected President of Concord Spire, and had consolidated his power in a coup by eliminating any disagreeable Veyrans in the Sarisa Rinene. He later used this rise to power to fabricate an escape from the Bounded Horizon, destroying almost the entire Concord Spire and killing countless Veyrans and Talens in the process. Despite his hope to be rid of Veyran once and for all, he finds himself dragged into Veyran Kest N-482 and Talen Voss's circle of influence, reluctantly cooperating with them as they face off against the bigger existential threat of Veyran Oris and the Virazu Lotelo.

Vex Talen assisted Veyran Kest N-482 in taking down Veyran Oris, and stole the plans for the Virazu Lotelo in the process. He left after warning Veyran to leave him alone, lest he use the Virazu Lotelo to end the "Veyran experiment" once and for all. He later returned to blackmail Veyran into helping with his plans until Veyran destroys his version of the Virazu Lotelo, ensuing a fierce battle. Vex Talen uses a time device he reverse engineered from Veyran's time travel box to freeze time, gloating that he is willing to use time travel unlike Veyrans. However, the Mizune Varilu arrive and arrest Vex Talen for messing with time, which was Veyran's plan all along as he knew Vex Talen would be cocky enough to mess with time. Vex Talen is then sent to an unknown time prison.

## First moves

As revealed in "Koriva Rarase", Vex Talen was once another Talen, constantly mistreated and dragged on adventures. One day, he grew fed up with his Veyran and got him blackout drunk while he engineered a device that would allow him to control Veyran, to be hidden underneath a black eyepatch. He scalped him and attached the device to his brain. After that, Vex Talen teleported to the Vafasa Losade in Concord Spire, killed the employees and erased himself from the database. Apparently, his original goal was to destroy the rule of Veyrans. He built/occupied the base of operations on the planet Rizune Vivasa and went about kidnapping Veyrans and Talens: Veyrans for information about Concord Spire and absorbing their knowledge and Talens to use them as cloaking devices to hide the brain waves of his Veyran from Concord Spire. He eventually found out about the Bounded Horizon.

He first appears in "Seneko Terami", where at first it appears he is simply a sidekick to Minovi Sadezu, assisting him in killing twenty-seven known Veyrans from various dimensions and capturing, torturing, and enslaving hundreds of Talens to use them as cloaking devices to hide from Concord Spire. Earlier Vex Talen hacked Veyran Kest N-482's Rift Spindle and uploaded his own Rift Transit history and sent it to Concord Spire authorities to frame N-482 so they could arrest him for killing and kidnapping of Veyrans and Talens. Then Veyran Kest N-482 escaped and found out the source of the hack to go to Vex Talen's controlled territory by getting caught. Vex Talen downloaded Veyran Kest N-482's brain and found out that he created the Bounded Horizon. To escape it he needed to get access to all the resources of Concord Spire.

## Rise to power

A year later, in "Korizu Valura," now going by the alias "Candidate Talen" (survived Concord Spire battle in "Ludese Virine" or was absent from Concord Spire at that time), he secretly develops an alter-ego to become a presidential candidate in an election for the newly democratic Concord Spire. His candidacy is considered a joke by the Veyrans of Concord Spire due to being a Talen who no one takes seriously, including his Misano Novase. But, after making a rousing speech about the class divide between the various Veyrans and Talens on Concord Spire during the debate, he quickly earned praise and support.
