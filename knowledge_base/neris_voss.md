# Neris Voss

## Overview

She is a member of the Voss household and the daughter of Daren Voss and Selra Voss/Selra Aster, the older sister of Talen Voss, and the mother of Vinofa Zuluri.

She currently acts as the older sister and the granddaughter of Talen and Veyran, respectively, from the Raralo Delone and Reality N-482. For a time, she was the only other known member of the the Voss household to be aware of that fact.

She and Talen often squabble and compete for Veyran's affection, but also maintain a unique bond stemming from the revelation that the Talen she's with is not her original brother.

## Appearance

Neris is a female teenager of average height, thin build, and wide hips. She has a fair complexion, shoulder-length ginger hair, an oblong-shaped face, and a pointed nose. Her most common outfit has been a bright pink tank-top, white capri-pants, and black slip-on shoes. She usually has her hair pulled back in a ponytail. Notably, she shares the exact same face as her father, Daren.

Her pajamas consist of a white under and pink shorts more often than a pink tank top with red and white striped sweatpants.

## Personality

Neris has some personality traits of a typical Averan teenager, as she wishes to be popular among her peers, and finds the antics of her family to be dysfunctional, though she still cares for them. She's very attached to her phone and social media, taking videos of her dog Frostveil when he became super-smart, and shuddering at the thought of her father Daren joining VelaNet.

It is revealed that Neris can be jealous of the close relationship about her grandfather Veyran and her brother Talen, and she can sometimes feel ignored by her grandfather. While Veyran tries to keep her at a distance, the two of them have gone on their own adventures, including on the planet Zharuun. Her complex of being ignored by Veyran exhibits into a connection with the Desera Radeva in "Delune Saseri".

Although Neris is not as scientifically-minded as her grandfather Veyran, she does possess intelligence. Neris is shown to be a very smart and nimble-witted teen, at least compared to rest of her family. Currently, she is not as violent as Selra was when Selra was young, but enjoys fighting alongside Veyran and reacts quickly to life-or-death situations.

She is also a sociopathic and narcissistic person, like her grandfather; similar to the way that Talen responds, but only when truly pushed to his limits. Neris, however, seems more readily able to access these elements of her deeper-rooted personality when subjected to her grandfather Veyran's influence for a relatively short amount of time, as seen in "Luvira Denozu".

She is also shown to possess a strong desire for family. Her stance towards them is best summarized in her quote from "Loneri Deriri", where she explains that she would rather live an isolated existence without a future if it means that the family won't have to abandon Veyran, saying that she believes that, "you don't love someone in hopes of a reward". In "Mimimi Visako", she is shown to reiterate this sentiment when she freely runs away with the family to a cabin in the woods so that Selra will never have to experience her existential crisis alone.

## Veyran

Neris seems jealous of the fact that her grandfather Veyran chooses to go on adventures with her brother Talen and not her. Veyran claims that the opinions of his granddaughter Neris mean "very little" to him, but he still cares for her, as he admits that the top Neris wears in "Neteri Dekova" is cute.

Neris gets a part-time job at a cursed antique store run by the Desera Radeva, aka Desera Radeva, just to spite Veyran, in "Delune Saseri". Despite this, Veyran helps his granddaughter Neris buff up and take revenge on Desera Radeva after they get fired by him. Afterwards, the two go around beating up other people doing bad things.

In "Tedete Fafate", Neris, along with her brother and grandfather, freezes time to stop her parents from finding their house in wretched shape. She proceeds to have fun together all around town, doing things like carving pumpkins, pulling peoples' pants down, and watching the movie Sesako Zuvivi together.
