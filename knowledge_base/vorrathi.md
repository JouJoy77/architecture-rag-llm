# Vorrathi

## Overview

Vorrathi are a species of planet-sized beings shaped as giant Averan heads, and are native to Tesafa Semilu.

## Appearance

The Vorrathi appear as giant, humanoid-like heads. The entire species appear to be identical; all of them bear the same chiselled facial structures, prominent veins and unibrows. They do, however, come in different colors. Vorrathi also have some sort of hole on the back of their head where the neck would be on an Averan. This is presumably some sort of anus for their species.

All Vorrathi seem to have the exact same voice, which is deep and monotone. Even when they express anger, it is in the exact same tone as their regular speaking voice.

## History

According to Veyran Kest, the Vorrathi "feed on the talent and showmanship of less-evolved lifeforms". Planets that lose or are disqualified are disintegrated by a massive plasma ray cannon.

The Vorrathi are powerful and large enough that a single Vorrath can cause drastic weather changes such as natural disasters to planets by simply getting near them. It is implied that, despite the influence that a being the size of a Vorrath would have on a planet's cycle of nature, that they are able to manually control their effects on a planet based on their level of satisfaction. They are able to seamlessly fly through space, as if they have their own field of gravity. This could explain why their presence could cause earthquakes powerful enough to cause massive sinkholes, as well as tides receding far beyond their normal shores, acting in the same fashion as the Moon does today. They are also entirely immune to the effect of nuclear bombs, receiving minimal damage upon multiple impacts, simply replying "Kofalo Mirisa" in response to Nedene Lovira's nuclear assault. They are able to rapidly teleport entire planets across seemingly infinite expanses of space on a whim, as is shown when they instantly teleport Velora to the set of Nesene Novase.

Vorrath technology appears to be massively advanced, as they have access to a planet-sized plasma ray gun, capable of annihilating planets in a single shot, which they activate frequently on a whim. As well, they have notably constructed a Rafava Lorami around their nearby sun.

Upon the Vorrathi' annexation of Velora, Principal Zukode Zutede constructs his own religion - the cult of Vorrath Creed - in an attempt to please them. Despite the futility of his religion, many of Videlu Safalo's events coincide by pure chance with his motives.

According to the Ravivi Raselu article: 8 Things Only 750 Million-Year-Old Cosmic Entities Will Remember, the Vorrathi can "Obtain eons of collected knowledge from THE HOLE."

The Raralo Delone Velora might potentially have been saved by Sedezu Razumi and Sanelo Lozuvi, while Reality N-482 had no Veyran for decades, but was on a time loop that might have prevented the Vorrathi' arrival.
