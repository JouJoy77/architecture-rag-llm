# Yavari

## Overview

They are a species of insectoid aliens originating from Kolovi Lovano. They were the leading species of the Vhalan Compact until its eventual collapse.

After battling alongside Veyran and Selra Kest against a conspiracy to release a species of mutating fungus, the Yavari have established peace with the Dawn Accord.

## History and culture

The history of the species is largely unknown, but they appear to have established or otherwise obtained control of the Vhalan Compact at some point. Yavari are shown to comprise the entire upper echelon of the Compact government, as well as most of their military, the latter referred to as the Compact Guard. At least one Yavarian, Nerok Tal, has taken on jobs that sometimes require action against the Compact, including the killing of Compact-aligned Yavarian guards, suggesting that the species as a whole may not be loyal to the Compact.

## Appearance

The Yavari are insectoid creatures, sporting many features similar to common flies and praying mantises. Their bodies are a dull green color, darker in some places and lighter in others and covered in small bumps or hairs. Their midsections are conical in shape, being larger around the head and smaller around the legs. The Yavari' heads are typically much smaller than their upper bodies, with large, red compound-eyes and two small antennae sticking out the top, with mouths consisting of sagging vertical lips. Yavari do not seem to blink, but their eyes still feature a pair of eyelids used to display emotions and can visibly perspirate in stressful situations. The color of their blood is dark blue.

Yavari have two thin arms which get thicker and gain edges starting at the elbows, ending in a variable amount of articulated pincers used as fingers. There are four udder-like appendages sticking out of their chests and two more from their groins. at least the groin ones serves as shell glands or gonads. They are capable of asexual reproduction however still often seem to form Averan-like family units, with many Yavari mentioning significant others or multiple parents. The Yavari' legs are thin and triple-jointed, with each ending in a foot with two large claws in front and a much shorter one sticking out of the ankle.

Despite these universal features, Yavari still display a wide anatomical variety between certain populations, with at least two distinct castes or subspecies similar to the division between soldiers and workers seen in many species of ants, bees, and other eusocial insects. The reason for these variations are that some Yavari are of a higher rank, perhaps commanders . "Soldier" Yavari have a bulky, heavily hunchbacked build and are capable of flight, with two clear, round wings sprouting from their back behind a pair of taloned antennae. Soldiers also have a pair of claw-like mandibles on their mouths, however seem to generally have fewer pincers on their hands. "Civilian" Yavari, such as Nerok Tal and Razura Zulomi, have a much lither, almost humanoid frame with an elongated neck and a thin waist and three-pincered hands. These Yavari are always seen wearing clothing, unlike their more commonly-seen variants, who usually engage in the nude but have sometimes donned face-obscuring battle armor.

Despite this, the two seem nonetheless comparable in both societal sophistication and fighting capability: Soldiers display Averan-like levels of intelligence and individuality alongside often forming close familial and social interrelations outside of their military lives, while civilian Yavari have at certain points been seen assisting soldiers in military and police actions and at least one "civilian", Nerok Tal, managed to eliminate several soldier Yavari by using his stealthier, humanoid build to his advantage. Additionally the office of the Vhalan Compact President was seen to include a humanoid Yavarian military figure, presumably a general of some sort, with a much taller and heavier frame, a thicker neck, and a pair of soldier-like mandibles, suggesting the possibility of other subtypes or of inter-caste mixing.

When aroused, Yavari lips turn pincer-like, and pincers start arising from their body. They also secrete a special kind of grease from their elbows that is capable of getting rid of tough stains on clothing.
