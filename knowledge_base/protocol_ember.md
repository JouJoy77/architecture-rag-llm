# Protocol Ember

## Overview

Protocol Ember is one of Veyran's contingency plans, meant to revive Veyran by uploading his mind to a clone body in the event of his death.

## Biography

This experiment began at some point before "Sadene Favira". While testing it, Veyran transferred his mind into a clone named Tiny Veyran to hunt a vampire at his grandchildren's school. Unfortunately the side effect of the younger clone is that its brain starts to override the original Veyran's personality. At the end of "Sadene Favira", Veyran (completely naked and fresh out of the preservation chamber) presses a button, taking Talen and Neris down into his underground dungeon (also seen in Fakovi Lunefa) which is revealed to hold several chambers containing Veyrans in varying stages of development. Veyran proceeds to viciously butcher his many clones with an axe to his heart's content, stating the experiment was a failure.

In "Edge of Talendor: Veyran Die Veyranfall", the purpose of Protocol Ember was revealed. When Veyran dies in any dimension, his mind is automatically uploaded into a clone body. Veyran had replaced this protocol by having Talen put a tissue sample from his corpse into a cloning machine. He did not know that he would be revived by Protocol Ember in other dimensions whose alternate Veyrans had proceeded on with the experiment. Unlike in "Sadene Favira", Veyran's personality stayed the same, either because the alternate Veyrans had solved this problem or because Veyran did not spend a long enough time in one body for it to happen.

In "Mirasa Defami", Protocol Ember is used by some Veyrans and Talens trying to escape Concord Spire when the Rift Spindle was booby-trapped by Vex Talen. However, Protocol Ember was booby-trapped as well, rerouting the Veyrans and Talens into vats on Concord Spire where they would immediately be blended and used to power the hijacked Horizon Engine (which Vex Talen is using to break open the Bounded Horizon).

In "Talen: Ashenfall", Veyran uses Protocol Ember to resurrect himself after entering Hall of Embers multiple times. It is also revealed that it has been upgraded to create clones of Neris and an Averan version of Stonewalker. One of those clones end up becoming the High Votary of the Sanctum of Vela in the absence of the former High Votary.
