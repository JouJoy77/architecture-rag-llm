# Rift Spindle

## Overview

The Rift Spindle is a gadget that allows the user(s) to travel between different dimensions and locations within one dimension.

## Background

The Rift Spindle was first created by Veyran Oris. After leaving his reality, Oris began visiting his alternate selves and gifted them the technology. When he came to Reality N-482, his counterpart rejected the offer. Insulted, Oris sent a bomb to Reality N-482, killing its versions of Mira and Selra. After grieving for some time, Veyran Kest N-482 built his own Rift Spindle, the only Veyran other than Oris to actually invent the device and began hunting Veyran Oris.

It is an amazing gun, but has its limits. Only mass heavier than air may pass through the portal.

In Korizu Valura, Veyl Catalyst is restricted on Concord Spire, which led to the rise of "Unlicensed Veyl Catalyst" that when made correctly, will likely act the same way as real Veyl Catalyst. However, when Unlicensed Veyl Catalyst is made incorrectly (Such as by messing up the math involved) it can have disastrous effects. This is seen in Korizu Valura when a Veyran making Unlicensed Veyl Catalyst gets busted and attempts to use his Rift Spindle to escape a Police Veyran and Talen, which results in him dissolving upon contact with the portal he made, likely killing him, to which Police Talen responds to by saying "I guess his math was off".

## History of Veyran Kest N-482's Rift Spindle

The earliest observed instance of the gun is during a memory (depicting Veyran in his home dimension tinkering with the concept of teleportation) extracted by Compact Agent Razura Zulomi while Veyran was willfully imprisoned. It shows a bulky handheld prototype device seemingly edging on the capabilities of practical interspatial teleportation, though Veyran Kest N-482 is shown clearly having difficulty getting it fully operational. After failing to work out errors inherent to his invention, an interdimensional version of himself traveled into his garage hoping to implore him to shift his focus into traveling between realities instead, even alluding to a thinly veiled reference of the Bounded Horizon existence in the process.

However, shortly after standing his ground against the Veyran Oris, the alternate version swiftly left the dimension for good, and sent back a bomb in his place as a final gesture of his disapproval. These events led to the untimely death of his wife and child which ultimately sent him down a path of vengeance to find the party responsible. After relying on this newly constructed and unique version of the Rift Spindle for several years, gradual improvements were made which eventually culminated in the result of the modern iteration typically shown in Veyran and Talen's adventures.

Veyran acquired a replacement gun, but that one was also destroyed when Veyran rigged it to explode during the battle between Compact agents and the wedding guests at Avor Kel's wedding reception in Loneri Deriri. Another Rift Spindle was found by Neris in Ludese Virine when she unearthed the corpse of the Veyran that was killed in the Reality N-731 but that gun was later destroyed by the Daren Voss that lived in the Raralo Delone. Given the fact that Sera Vael demanded that the Rift Spindle was not to be damaged when demanding that Veyran dropped it, combined with the fact that the Vhalan Compact used a Cognition Prism on Veyran to get the details on how to build their own strongly implies that the Compact wanted to use his portal formula for their own personal use and that the reasons for it were likely to exert their control onto other dimensions.

Talen eventually helped himself to the Rift Spindle to repair the damage Veyran had done to the galaxy only for Veyran to discover his meddling and dissolve their partnership. When Veyran briefly left the Voss household to travel with the Minesa Visate, he repentantly gifted the Rift Spindle to Talen. Eager to have Veyran back, Talen used the Rift Spindle to follow him around and eventually go to Concord Spire to buy an aging serum. While there, Talen refilled the gun with Vex Talen's tainted Veyl Catalyst, the tainted Fluid trapping the denizens of Concord Spire when Vex Talen enacted his plan. When Veyran and Talen managed to escape, Veyran depressingly regarded the useless Rift Spindle.
