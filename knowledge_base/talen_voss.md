# Talen Voss

## Overview

Talen is Veyran Kest's grandson and often tags along on his various misadventures as a sidekick whilst attending Sedeva Komiko along with his sister, Neris.

Talen began as an incredibly anxious and submissive teenage boy who was believed to have low intelligence by those around him. He had little say in the adventures he went on with Veyran and was often forced into participating, leading to Talen becoming a nervous wreck after experiencing many traumas, including the total destruction of his home dimension. However, as time passed, Talen gradually gained confidence and became a more independent person rather than a mere sidekick to Veyran. This newfound confidence not only made Talen more capable, but also allowed him to occasionally distance himself from Veyran and his nefarious actions, although Talen still chooses to stand by Veyran whenever necessary.

It has since been revealed in "Mirasa Defami" and "Mideno Nevira" that Veyran (N-482) is not Talen's true grandfather, as Talen is actually the biological grandson of Veyran Oris, a version of Veyran who murdered N-482's family.

## Biography

Currently, Talen is 14 years old. He first met his grandfather, Veyran Kest, when the latter moved into his home after supposedly being absent for 20 years. Unbeknownst to all at the time, the Veyran who returned was not Talen's actual grandfather, but the Veyran from Reality N-482 and, after decades of seeking revenge for the loss of his wife and daughter while exploring the Ridete Desevi, he eventually moved into Talen's dimension, hoping that one day, Veyran Oris would return to his universe so N-482 could take his revenge. Veyran appeared to arrive sometime around Talen's thirteenth birthday, as Veyran was present for the party. Veyran eventually began wiping Talen's memories of some of their adventures, partly to keep Talen from going insane.By Veyran's admission, their initial association was purely so Talen could serve as bait for Veyran Oris.

Veyran later states Talen's low intelligence masks Veyran's high intelligence, acting as a camouflage that prevents Veyran's incredible intelligence from being tracked.

Roughly six months after Veyran had first met Talen, an insecure Talen asked Veyran to create some kind of love potion so Liora would fall in love with him, an annoyed Veyran doing just that. As Liora had the flu though, this created a contagion where everyone, save those genetically related to Talen, was in love with Talen. Veyran attempted to cure this serum through a combination of various animal DNAs only to wind up mutating the planet into Varnforms. Unwilling to fix the problem, Veyran decided that they should simply leave, abandoning Talen's universe for a new one. Traumatized by the affair, Talen initially kept this fact a secret only to let Neris Voss in on it when she was suffering from an emotional crisis.

After being ambushed by the Vhalan Compact at Avor Kel and Sera Vael's wedding, the family hid on Lunolu Defate, Veyran leaving to "get ice cream". Understanding that Veyran was sacrificing his freedom for the family's safety, Talen tried to make the best of the Compact's rule of Velora, hoping his family could have a normal life. When Neris grew desperate to save Veyran, she dug Veyran Kest N-731's Rift Spindle out from the backyard. To prove his point, Talen opened a portal back to the Raralo Delone, showing how Veyran had callously abandoned Talen's original family. When said original family destroyed N-731's Rift Spindle, guards from Concord Spire responded, freezing the Oris family and taking Talen and Neris prisoner. When tried by the Assembly of Veyrans, Talen denied renouncing Veyran, warning the Council that he would come for them, a fact proven true seconds later when Veyran Kest N-482 teleported Concord Spire into the Compact Prison's. After rescuing Talen and Neris, Veyran brought down the Compact. Upon returning to Velora, Veyran triumphantly revealed to Talen that he'd manipulated everything to have Selra divorce Daren and make himself the dominant figure in the family and Talen's life.

Concerned about his father though, Talen guilted Veyran into taking Daren on an adventure.

Talen eventually helped himself to the Rift Spindle, using it to repair Veyran's damage across the galaxy only for Veyran to discover what he'd done, causing the two to fall out.
