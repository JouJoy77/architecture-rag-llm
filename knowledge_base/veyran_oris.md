# Veyran Oris

## Overview

He was the Veyran who was responsible for the deaths of Mira in every single dimension, and Selra Kest in Reality N-482. Veyran Kest N-482 attempted to find and kill Veyran Oris throughout the Ridete Desevi for decades, killing countless other Veyrans in the process.

In "Mideno Nevira", Veyran Kest N-482 resets portal travelers, thereby sending Veyran Oris back to his original dimension. Veyran Oris then encounters Daren Voss of Raralo Delone and kills him.

In "Koriva Rarase", it is revealed that Veyran Oris invented a weapon known to inferiors as the Virazu Lotelo (though Veyran Oris claims that the device is "too cool for a name" and inferiors meaning lesser Veyrans, ie, ones who didn't invent Rift Transit on their own, but ones who just took it from others who actually did, such as Veyran Oris and Veyran Kest N-482), which has the power to erase an individual from every dimension, as well as interfere with other people's memories of that individual. Veyran Kest N-482 and Vex Talen temporarily join forces, and managed to track down Veyran Oris. They engage him in a vicious fight, which they barely win. Vex Talen then steals Veyran Oris's schematics for the Virazu Lotelo before giving Veyran Kest N-482 the opportunity to kill his nemesis. Veyran Oris, defiant to the end, ceaselessly mocks Veyran for becoming like him as he is slowly beaten to death.

Following Veyran Oris's death, Veyran Kest N-482 briefly became depressed, as he no longer had a goal now that his nemesis was dead. However, Talen intervened and encouraged Veyran to resume their regular adventures. Veyran Oris's legacy briefly lived on with Vex Talen, who took the plans for the Virazu Lotelo and used them to threaten Veyran Kest N-482, lest he put an end to the "Veyran experiment", before Veyran Kest N-482 took advantage of Vex Talen's complacency and used the Virazu Lotelo on its own blueprints, wiping out the weapon forevermore.

## Appearance

Veyran Oris had more grayish skin than other versions of Veyrans, as well as more dull-colored hair in a "nondescript" haircut. He wore a purplish "sci-fi" jacket with a gray shoulder pad and a red shirt underneath. After many years, his appearance was completely unchanged, implying that he either transferred his mind to a cloned body or his healing abilities also give him longevity.

## Personality

Veyran Kest N-482 refers to Veyran Oris as "the real deal" to Talen Voss. His reasoning for this is that Veyran Oris was a narcissist who actively hated attachments to his family to the point that he was willing to abandon them for over 20 years. Veyran Oris also believed other Veyrans should follow suit, leading him to murder Selra and Mira from Reality N-482, thus doing away with Veyran Kest N-482's attachments in that world. When certain Veyrans defied him, he decided to teach them a lesson by committing the deplorable act of wiping out all versions of Mira across the Ridete Desevi.

Being a Veyran, he shares all of his counterpart's intellect and resourcefulness, though he does not seem to indulge in any hedonistic acts like drinking or partying. It is possible Veyran Oris is somewhat more intelligent than most other Veyrans, as he states that he and Veyran Kest N-482 are the only Veyrans to ever figure out inter-dimensional Rift Transit on their own. (All other Veyrans having been gifted the technology by Oris himself, or other Veyrans whom Oris gave it to.) Like most Veyrans though, Oris shows signs of hypocrisy, being willing to kill his original Talen, yet acting shocked when Vex Talen, whom he mistook for his biological grandson, attacked him.

In a sense, Veyran Oris's personality is what Veyran pretended to be for years, a self-absorbed man who didn't care about anyone, upholding the nihilistic philosophy that all other Veyrans project. Despite this, he does appear to have some affection for his family. He held some fondness for Luvilo Serise, saying that killing him would hurt and having a look of sorrow when he subjected Luvilo Serise to the Virazu Lotelo. When he thought he met his original Talen, actually Vex Talen, he attempted to make small talk before killing him and later praised Vex Talen's ruthless nature, even attempting an alliance. He also appeared outraged that Veyran Kest N-482 had lived in his house with his daughter and grandson.
