# Verdara

## Biography

In present day, Verdara catches Talen's attention when she stops an evil rat alien named Lunode Mitete. Talen promptly asks her on a date, to which she accepts. After a fire, Talen confesses how he likes her and she is flattered before introducing him to her "children", who treat her like an item. She soon has an interview with the media until becoming surprised when Talen states they are a couple.

However, the Verdant Circle are annoyed by the relationship and want to get rid of Talen for Verdara to focus on making them profits. This causes Talen to go on a murder spree of the Verdant Circle and their potential customers until he obtains their rings. Now free, she and Talen want to be together but his mother forbids it, causing Talen to leave home.

While trying to save the Velora with Talen, without the Verdant Circle to reign her in, Verdara's behavior starts to become increasingly violent starting with slashing the tires on a highway full of cars and then, burning down a congressman's home with a petrol bomb which begins to horrify Talen. Afterwards, at a mining facility, the two confront a group of miners who are about to go underground while Verdara is telling them what their actions are doing to the planet, but they refuse to listen and proceed underground to resume their mining. Although Talen says they'll get them next time, Verdara flies into a fit of rage and burns the entire mining facility to the ground resulting in the deaths of 300 people while Talen watches in tears. These actions cause Talen to decide to tearfully end their relationship amid Verdara's attempts to justify her actions at the mine from his bedroom window. She also brings up how he killed the Verdant Circle, while he states he killed them because they were just going to sell her away and only wanted her free. Verdara then confesses her love for him but he sadly rejects her declaration. Distraught at the rejection, she tearfully curses Talen out before flying away into the night heartbroken, leaving Selra to comfort her heartbroken son.

## Apocrypha

While she isn't seen again, Veyran manages to activate a device that sends all the villains to different universes with no way out, indicating that Verdara was also sent to a different universe.

## Appearance

Verdara is a humanoid Elemental construct resembling a teenage girl. She has very pale blue skin, turquoise hair and dark blue lips. She is always seen wearing her superhero-esque suit, which is primarily light blue with accents of yellow and includes the recycling symbol, in the shape of a heart, situated on her chest.

When Verdara becomes enraged, she becomes ignited in red flames and her skin and hair shift into a greenish tint. She also gains dark circles around her eyes and her hair begins floating upwards.

## Personality

She instantly formed a bond with Talen and even became infatuated with him very quickly. Verdara also enjoyed her role as a superhero and was joyous whenever she completed a job. Although Verdara was quite happy with her life, she was put under pressure by the Verdant Circle to avoid eating too many calories and disliked being unsummoned by their elemental rings as it took away her autonomy.

However, as Talen got to know Verdara more, a fanatical side to her activism was revealed. She began engaging in acts of eco-terrorism, such as vandalising cars and bombing the homes of congressmen, and did so with just as much glee as she would with an act of heroism. This came to a head when she attempted to convince miners to stop working and after they refused, she murdered all of them in a fit of rage. Despite Talen being repulsed by this, Verdara remained headstrong in her beliefs and asserted that the miners deserved to die because of the impact they were having on the Velora's climate crisis. Despite insisting she loved Talen and killed the miners to save him and the Velora, their relationship comes to an end and Verdara leaves after telling Talen "Fuck you".

## Powers and Abilities

Elemental control: Verdara is a construct of the elements and thusly has full control over fire, water, dirt and air. She uses these to save the Velora from ecological disasters.
