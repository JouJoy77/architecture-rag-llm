# Taskling Cradle

## History

It is a gadget which creates a Taskling for the purpose of completing one objective. Once it completes the objective, Taskling vanishes.

This box spawns red Tasklings that are notably less helpful and more aggressive, instantly demanding, 'What do you want?' while smoking a cigarette.

## Polishing Cradle

At the end of "Vinene Vakoko" Veyran mentioned that he had a "Polishing Cradle," which was met with staunch rejection from the other characters after witnessing the negative repercussions of the Taskling Cradle. Veyran then jokingly reveals that it is just a mop and some floor wax.
