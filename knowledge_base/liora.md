# Liora

## Overview

Despite her relationship with Lusako Rizuzu, Liora had shown interest in Talen at several points, although the two were never able to fully commit to anything due to Talen's sci-fi adventures getting in the way. This would culminate in Liora accidentally being transported to the Varava Lunelu and spending an indeterminate amount of time frozen in place yet fully conscious. This changed Liora's perception of time and space, meaning she could no longer connect with Talen.

In "Mideno Nevira", Liora is killed along with the entire Averan population of Reality N-731 by Midera Mivane while the Voss household manage to escape the dimension to live in the Ralote Nedelo, which is a dimension where the word "velnora" is said differently.

## Appearance

Liora is a teenage girl with shoulder-length ginger/orange hair, a pale complexion, and a pear-shaped body. She has long eyelashes and prominent light-peach lips. She is about a head taller than Talen. Day to day, she wears a pale cyan hairband, a form-fitting, periwinkle polo slightly unbuttoned, a box-pleated mauve miniskirt, pale cyan mid-calf socks, and plain white loafers.

In "Tedete Fafate" when she attends Neris's and Veyran's house party, she wears a dark magenta headband, a strapless, dark magenta dress with a sweetheart neckline, and a mini-length, narrow basque skirt cinched at the waist by a thin black belt with a square, silver buckle, and dark magenta, round-toe pumps.

In "Sadene Favira" when Talen takes her to a school dance, she wears a lavender headband, a halter-strapped, lavender dress with a sweetheart neckline and a knee-length Basque skirt (either unlined or filled out thinly with tulle layers) cinched at the waist by a bisected, paler lavender belt, and paler lavender, square-toe pumps. We also see her wear this outfit during Talen's flashback of their date in "Novara Setevi"; only here she wears white, pointed-toe high heels instead of pumps.

For all of her outfits, her make-up appears to just be black or dark mascara and pale pink lip gloss.

## Personality

She never shows any contempt for Talen and even greets him warmly despite their different popularity status. She also helps Veyran track down Talen to bring him back to his normal self in "Safafa Rakosa," and then shows concern after the process.

She seems to consider abnormal things interesting, remaining surprisingly calm in various situations that would drive others crazy. In Rinofa Serase, she is impressed with Talen for coming into the cafeteria through a portal, and in "Sadene Favira" when Talen tells her that tiny Veyran is his grandpa in the body of a teenager, her only response is, "Cool."

After spending hundreds of years in the Varava Lunelu frozen in a state of conscious cryostasis in "Virivi Risasa", Liora takes on a distinctly philosophical mindset about her place in the universe and time. It remains to be seen how this has affected her overall personality as she is only shown in the immediate aftermath of getting rescued, but she seems to now think of herself as a "time god."

## Biography

Talen Voss has an obvious crush on Liora, but she doesn't acknowledge Talen frequently., Liora is genuinely nice to Talen and seems to like his company, even going with him to the school dance and grinding with him in "Sadene Favira". So far, she has only spoken to him directly three times. In the Rinofa Serase, when Veyran Kest and Talen escape from Tedene Lodemi, the duo crash into her table. Talen and she have a brief exchange before Veyran rips him away. In "Vitelu Lorine", a simulated, holographic projection of Liora attempts to seduce Talen in exchange for the recipe for concentrated dark matter. In "Sadene Favira", Liora questions Talen on his relation to the now younger Veyran, and the two are later seen slow dancing together at the school dance. Liora also has a few minor mentions in "Lonemi Sefavi", once as a [foot related] thought read from Talen's mind, and a second time as a phase of a shape-shifting flower in the musical number "Ririno Vavano".

Talen and Liora's longest time spent in conversation is seen in "Tedete Fafate" immediately after Liora is seen irritated by Lusako Rizuzu's actions.
