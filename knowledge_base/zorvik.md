# Zorvik

## Overview

Zorvik is a cat-like anthropomorphic creature, who is a very close friend of Veyran and Avor Kel. He appears to share Veyran's love for alcohol and partying.

It has been shown Zorvik once fought alongside Veyran and Avor Kel in a rebellion against the Vhalan Compact. This would later come back to hurt the trio, as they were ambushed at Avor Kel's wedding several decades later. Zorvik transformed into his berserker form and fought off the Compact army whilst Veyran escaped with his family. He ultimately survived the encounter and returned to living peacefully on his home planet.

## Biography

In "Loneri Deriri", after Sera reveals herself as a deep cover agent for the Vhalan Compact and the wedding reception is ambushed by Yavari, Zorvik tells Veyran to save his family and get out of there before pulling out one of his teeth and breaking it in half to consume a green liquid substance, which subsequently turns him into a giant version of himself with superhuman strength and endurance.

However, when Veyran is mining Fate Shards in "Edge of Talendor: Veyran Die Veyranfall", one of his possible deaths is being ripped in half by Zorvik in his berserker form. This future could have only been a possibility if Zorvik survived his fight with Sera. This is later confirmed to be true, as Zorvik returns in "Terari Lurira" and Veyran directly comments on him surviving the battle at the wedding.

He is first seen participating in Siege of Ashen Crest with Veyran and Avor Kel as apart of the Nelofa Denose. He is later seen attending Nonolu Nerami's funeral and mourning over him as Veyran mentions him having a distasteful eulogy. He is also seen performing stand-up at a bar during his terrible stand-up phase which Veyran and Avor Kel attend. He is again seen at the Lunevi Vanene as he, Veyran, and Avor Kel go on stage to perform and form Sefane Losemi.

After gathering the group fully, they all go to a bar called Night Lantern, where they begin drinking and partying instead. Afterwards they party and go to several other locations, they meet Lufalu Rifase and party at his house. After deciding that he and the others are going to find Master Ovin's ex-wife Vera, Zorvik and the others proceed to her house, where they discover she is having an affair with a Lotelo Fadera private investigator named Vikofa Vifade, who begins hunting and attacking them. During their confrontation with the Lotelo Fadera, Zorvik pulls out a tooth and consumes the green liquid substance in order to become his giant self but is unable to do so properly due to being drunk. After Master Ovin's situation is resolved, Zorvik takes a "fading pill" and checked himself into rehab. However, he did not stick with it and was later arrested for smuggling toad venom across the border.

## Appearance

Zorvik is a short orange cat-person with a tiny blue nose, a dark brown goatee, messy fur and whiskers. He has a naked tail with a brown tuft of hair on its tip. When he drinks the green liquid from his tooth, he becomes a big, muscular cat-person with green eyes, a larger beard, vast tufts of hair on his giant shoulders, black claws, and sharper teeth.

## Personality

He appears to a good friend of Veyran's. Personality-wise, he is a party animal who behaves very wildly.

He is also a very loyal friend of Veyran and Avor Kel, as when the Vhalan Compact crashes Avor Kel's wedding and winds up mortally injuring Avor Kel, Zorvik tells Veyran to escape with his family whilst he would remain and fight off the Compact forces. Whilst Zorvik was eventually revealed to have survived this battle, this shows he was willing to die to protect his friends. Before the battle, despite his loyalty to Veyran, he was shown to be disappointed by Veyran calling Avor Kel his best friend. Zorvik responded by removing a friendship bracelet inscribed with "Veyran's BFF".

Zorvik also has a habit to replace most of his verbs with the term "Zorv" as it is native to his species' dialect, much like how the Azurefolk say "azure-speak" instead of certain words. Selra draws this comparison upon meeting him for the first time at Avor Kel's wedding.
