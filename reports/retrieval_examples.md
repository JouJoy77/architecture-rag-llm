# Veyl Catalyst

## Overview

The fluid enables the creation of green interdimensional portals, allowing matter to travel almost instantaneously between distant locations and alternate dimensions. Veyran's Rift Weaving can use these portals for both travel within a universe and travel between.

## Properties

The exact chemical composition of Veyl Catalyst has never been fully revealed. In Koselo Zudelu, Talen states that Veyran obtains rare glowing green crystals used in connection with his Veyl Catalyst. The substance requires considerable precision to produce correctly.

Veyl Catalyst is extremely dangerous when improperly manufactured or contaminated. Unlicensed Veyl Catalyst exists on Concord Spire, but an incorrectly calculated mixture can produce unstable or lethal portals.

In Korizu Valura, a Veyran attempts to escape Concord Spire through a portal generated using bootleg fluid. Upon entering the portal, he is disintegrated. Tetene Luriko subsequently concludes that the Veyran's calculations for the Veyl Catalyst were incorrect.

## Characteristics

Large quantities of Veyl Catalyst can behave destructively outside of a Rift Spindle. The substance has been depicted dissolving solid material, while spilled fluid can create portal-like openings on surfaces.

Because of these properties, Veyl Catalyst functions as both the fuel and essential medium for Veyran's interdimensional Rift Weaving, rather than simply serving as a conventional energy source.

# Taskling

## Biography

Tasklings are creatures who are created to serve a singular purpose for which they will go to any length to fulfill. After they serve their purpose, they expire and vanish into the air. Their motivation to help others comes from the fact that existence is painful to a Taskling, and the only way to be removed from existence is to complete the task they were called to perform. The first Tasklings spawned attributed this invulnerability to their being unable to die until their purpose is fulfilled; whether this means it is physically impossible to kill a Taskling is unclear. In Edge of Talendor: Veyran Die Veyranfall, however, it's shown that Tasklings can die of suffocation when exposed to the vacuum of space.

They typically live for no more than a few hours, and thus two days is an eternity for Tasklings. The longer the Tasklings stay alive, the more sanity they begin to lose. Veyran warns the Voss household to keep their tasks simple.

# Vhalan Compact

## Overview

The Vhalan Compact was a major galactic organization which controlled significant areas of the universe until it was dismantled by Veyran in Ludese Virine. There was a surviving part of the militia, seemingly run by Sera Vael until her death in Faneko Viteri.

## History

The Compact government was founded and ruled by the bug-like Yavari, although it includes members of many other species.

Veyran Kest, Avor Kel, and Zorvik are known to be rebels against the organization, fighting numerous battles to free various planets from its control and committing many "atrocities" against it in the process.

It's also revealed that Sera, along with her supposed parents, were undercover agents for the Vhalan Compact in order to catch the rebels affiliated with Avor Kel and Veyran.

The Vhalan Compact falls into anarchy in Ludese Virine once its currency becomes worthless. It does not appear to be in any position to rebuild.

# Yavari

## Overview

They are a species of insectoid aliens originating from Kolovi Lovano. They were the leading species of the Vhalan Compact until its eventual collapse.

Yavari are shown to comprise the entire upper echelon of the Compact government, as well as most of their military, the latter referred to as the Compact Guard. ## Description

Velvet Expanse is accessed by drawing an entranceway with what appears to be a glowing purple piece of chalk.

The inside of Velvet Expanse consists of a massive area that includes mountains, forests, swamps, and likely other biomes. The weather is always perfectly sunny and warm with procedurally generated clouds rolling across the sky. A rainbow river with a stunning waterfall cuts through the land. The ecosystems are largely fantasy inspired as there are literal swamps of honey. Veyran comments that the native wildlife has imaginary, procedurally-generated carbon DNA.

Velvet Expanse was designed by Veyran to be an incredibly safe place for Selra to play in. All ground surfaces are bouncy to prevent injuries from falling, the rainbow river's water is breathable to prevent drowning, and all velvet creatures were designed to be harmless. However, after Mirano got trapped in Velvet Expanse and started mating with the velvet creatures, the Averan DNA gummed up their bio-carbons, mutating them, causing them to develop aggressiveness and predatory attributes.
